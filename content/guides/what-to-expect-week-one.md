# What to Expect in Week One

The first seven days of a campaign carry the highest enquiry volume of the entire sale. Here's roughly what happens, and when.

## Day 1 — Listing live

- Property goes live on realestate.com.au and domain.com.au within hours of photos being approved
- A "Just Listed" notification fires to anyone with a saved search matching your suburb and price band
- Your portal updates with the first views within ~30 minutes

## Days 2–3 — The first wave

- Expect the bulk of week-one enquiries on the **second and third days**, not day one
- Most buyers will look at the photos before reading the description
- Saves are a better early signal than views — they indicate intent

## Day 4–5 — Inspection scheduling

- We'll start booking private inspections for serious buyers
- The first open home is usually on the first Saturday after listing
- If you've had unusually high views but low enquiries, we'll talk about pricing or photos

## Day 6–7 — First open home

- We'll capture group numbers, names, and interest levels
- A written summary lands in your portal that evening
- The Monday after the first open is the most useful conversation we have all week

## What's normal

- A 5–10× drop in views from week one to week two — this is expected and not a cause for concern
- Some enquiries that go quiet for a few weeks before re-emerging at the second or third open
