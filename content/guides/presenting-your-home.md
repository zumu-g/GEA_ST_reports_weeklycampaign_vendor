# Presenting Your Home at Opens

Buyers form an opinion within seconds of walking through the door. The good news: every one of those seconds is in your control.

## The hour before

- Open every internal door — visitors should never have to push or pull
- Open every blind and turn on every light, even in the middle of the day
- Light a single neutral candle in the main living area; nothing fragranced
- Empty bins, tuck shoes away, and clear bench tops in the kitchen
- Set the dining table for two — it implies the space is being lived in
- Adjust climate control to **22°C**

## During the open

- Leave the house — buyers won't speak freely if owners are present
- Take pets with you; even friendly animals reduce the time buyers spend inside
- Park your car off the property if the driveway can be used by buyers

## After the open

We'll send through a summary that evening: groups through, interest level, any feedback. If something needs adjusting before the next inspection, we'll talk about it then.

## What buyers consistently mention

- Natural light and a sense of space
- A clean, uncluttered kitchen
- A welcoming entry
- The street appeal as they approach
